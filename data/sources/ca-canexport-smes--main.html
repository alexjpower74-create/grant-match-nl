CanExport SMEs

Applications are not being accepted at this time

The application intake period ended at 12:00 p.m. (ET) on August 31, 2026. Information regarding future application intake periods will be posted on this page when available.

The CanExport SMEs (small and medium-sized enterprises) program offers competitive funding to help eligible Canadian small and medium-sized enterprises expand into new international markets.

The program supports businesses by sharing the costs of export-related activities, helping reduce risk and support export diversification. It is intended for companies with meaningful economic ties to Canada, the capacity to grow internationally, and a clear plan to export Canadian goods and services.

Successful projects may receive up to $50,000 in funding for international business development activities. Funding is limited, and applications are assessed on a rolling competitive basis.

Application processing

Service standards and current processing timelines vary depending on the target market identified in your application:

  • International markets outside the United States: Service standard of up to 60 business days. Complete applications submitted on or before August 13, 2026 are currently under review.
  • United States projects: Service standard of up to 90 business days. Complete applications submitted on or before June 18, 2026 are currently under review.

Processing times may vary depending on application volume and complexity.
