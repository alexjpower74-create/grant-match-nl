How Grant Match NL works

We read each program's own official page, save a copy, and quote it word for word. Your answers are checked against those quotes with fixed rules. No one guesses, and nothing is decided by a computer model.

The four labels

  • Looks like a fitEvery answer the page asks about matches, at least one of them is more than your location, and we checked the page in the last 60 days. Some items may still say "check this yourself".
  • Might fitSomething beyond your location matches and nothing rules you out, but something your answers could settle is Unknown, or the page needs checking again.
  • Not enough to go onNothing rules you out, but nothing beyond your location could be checked against the page, so there's no real sign either way.
  • Doesn't fitAt least one answer doesn't match what the page asks for, or the program is closed.

When a program takes applications is shown on its own line. It never changes the label. If the page doesn't say, call the office to confirm.

What "Unknown" means

Unknown is never counted as a match. On each program the Unknowns are split in two: the page doesn't say (its wording can't settle your answer) and we didn't ask you (things you can answer yourself: a business plan, a question you skipped, an exact figure). Each one means one of these:

  • Unknown: check this yourself: the page asks for something your answers can't settle, like having a business plan.
  • Unknown: you didn't answer this or weren't sure: you skipped a question the page cares about, or picked "Not sure".
  • Unknown: the page's wording doesn't settle it for your answer: the page's words could be read either way for what you picked, so we don't guess.
  • Unknown: your answer is close to the page's limit: your answer is a range, and the page's limit falls inside it.
  • Unknown: the page doesn't say: the official page doesn't state it, so we don't either.

Grant Match never applies for you

It doesn't send your answers anywhere, doesn't store them, and can't promise you qualify. Only the program's own office can say yes. Always read the official page before you apply.

Programs we left out, and why

Grant Match only uses pages it is allowed to quote.

  • BDC loans (Business Development Bank of Canada): their terms say you need written permission to copy or store anything from their website. BDC's terms of use
  • Futurpreneur (loans for young entrepreneurs): their terms say their website may not be copied or read by software. Futurpreneur's terms and conditions
  • Ulnooweg Development Group (financing for Indigenous businesses): their website publishes no terms and says "All rights reserved", so we have no permission to copy it. Ulnooweg's website (no terms page published)

You can still contact these organizations yourself.
